Clamp

THINK BEFORE YOU PASTE

A browser extension that warns you before sensitive data, like Social Security numbers, credit card numbers, API keys, or your own custom terms, gets pasted or typed into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, or Copilot.

Why it matters

It's easy to paste something into an AI chat without thinking twice. Clamp catches it in the moment, whether the sensitive text was pasted in or typed directly, and gives you a chance to reconsider before it's sent.

  • Detects Social Security numbers, credit card numbers, and API keys from major providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, AWS, GitHub, Google, Stripe, and more)
  • Works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Copilot
  • Warns you, never blocks you. You stay in control of what you send
  • Runs entirely on your device; nothing you paste is ever sent anywhere
