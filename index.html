Clamp

THINK BEFORE YOU PASTE

A browser extension that warns you before sensitive data, like Social Security numbers, credit card numbers, API keys, or your own custom terms, gets pasted or typed into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, or Copilot.

Here's what it actually catches

Three situations that happen more often than people realize.

LEGAL / ADMIN

A paralegal is asking an AI tool to help reformat an intake form.

Hi, following up on the intake form. Could you confirm the client's SSN is 219-09-9999? I want to make sure it matches what's on file before we submit the paperwork.
Clamp: that message looks like it may contain a Social Security number.
ENGINEERING

A developer is debugging a failing API call late at night.

I'm getting a 401 error when I call the endpoint. Here's my config: ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=sk-ant-api03-aBcDeFgHiJkLmNoPqRsTuVwXyZ123456789. Any idea what's wrong?
Clamp: that message looks like it may contain an API key or secret token.
BILLING / OPS

Someone is asking for help writing a payment follow-up email.

Please charge the card on file, 4532015112830366, for this month's invoice. Let me know once it goes through.
Clamp: that message looks like it may contain a credit card number.

None of these people meant any harm. They were just moving fast. That's the whole point: Clamp catches the moment before it becomes a problem, whether the text was pasted in or typed directly, and lets you decide what to do next.

Why it matters

  • Detects Social Security numbers, credit card numbers, and API keys from major providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, AWS, GitHub, Google, Stripe, and more)
  • Works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Copilot
  • Warns you, never blocks you. You stay in control of what you send
  • Runs entirely on your device; nothing you paste is ever sent anywhere
